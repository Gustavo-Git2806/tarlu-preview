Requirements & key information

What we need from you.

Before your branded packaging arrives at any Tarlu facility, please make sure the following requirements are met. Missing any of these will cause a delay at goods-in.

  • Packaging must be barcoded — one unique barcode per SKU / size variant.
  • Packaging must be visibly branded so it can be identified on-site without opening the box.
  • ASNs (advanced shipping notices) must be raised for every packaging delivery.
  • Packaging is checked in at our standard goods-in rates.
  • Packaging is not counted at unit level once inside the pallet.
  • Tarlu does not accept liability for loss of or damage to packaging.
  • Packaging is stored at our standard storage rate.
  • Processing fees apply per unit — see fee schedule opposite.
