Our capabilities

Complete fulfilment, end to end.

We don't just store and ship — we add value at every stage of your supply chain.

— Group 01

Inbound & storage

Goods arrive. We inspect, count, rack — and your stock is ready to sell.

  • Goods-in processing

    Thorough checking and system entry.

    Every inbound pallet is inspected, counted and reconciled against your ASN before it hits the racking. SKU-level accuracy from day one.

  • Secure storage

    Scalable, compliant warehousing.

    Ambient, bonded and food-grade storage across our UK and EU sites. Soil Association accredited, HMRC Fulfilment House registered.

  • Quality control

    Every product meets your standards.

    Custom QC checkpoints defined by you — from garment inspection to seal integrity. Rejected stock is quarantined and reported.

— Group 02

Value-added services

Kitting, custom packaging, freight forwarding, insurance — the extras that make your brand feel like a brand.

  • Assembly & kitting

    Combine SKUs into ready-to-ship kits.

    Bundle promotional sets, subscription boxes and PDQ displays. Kitting to spec, priced per unit or per hour.

  • Bespoke packaging

    Packaging that reflects your brand identity.

    In-house design and sourcing of custom mailer boxes, tissue paper, void fill and inserts. Sustainable options available.

  • Freight forwarding

    Simplify international logistics.

    Sea, air and road freight coordinated end-to-end. Customs clearance included, IOSS / OSS registered.

  • Goods-in-transit insurance

    Peace of mind, end to end.

    Comprehensive cover for stock while in our care and while in transit to your customer.

— Group 03

Pick, pack & dispatch

The core of every order. Fast, accurate, branded.

  • Order picking & packing

    Fast, accurate fulfilment.

    Real-time order sync from your ecommerce platform. Cut-off 3pm for same-day dispatch. 99.9% pick accuracy.

  • Gift boxing & personalisation

    Add a branded, premium touch.

    Handwritten notes, gift wrapping, monogramming and engraving — the finishing touches that turn buyers into fans.

  • Personalised despatch messages

    Custom communications on your behalf.

    Branded despatch confirmations, delivery updates and post-purchase flows sent from your domain.

  • Despatch & tracking

    Reliable courier integrations, real-time updates.

    Royal Mail, DPD, DHL, UPS, FedEx, Evri and Parcelforce — routed intelligently by weight, destination and service level.

— Group 04

Business support

Trading in the UK for the first time? We'll help you get set up.

  • UK VAT & EORI registration

    Get set up to trade in the UK.

    End-to-end registration support for overseas brands entering the UK market.

  • UK company formation

    Establish your UK entity.

    We introduce trusted partners for company incorporation, banking and accounting.

  • Import & customs compliance

    Trade with confidence post-Brexit.

    HS codes, duty relief, IPR, bonded warehousing — our team keeps your imports moving.
