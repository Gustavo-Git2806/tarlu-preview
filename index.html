Bonded facilities

HMRC-approved bonded warehouses across the UK and EU. Store duty-suspended goods legally, deferring VAT and import duties until despatch.

Soil Association accredited

Certified to store and handle organic products end to end. Full chain-of-custody documentation, segregated storage, licenced packaging.

HMRC Fulfilment House

Registered under the Fulfilment House Due Diligence Scheme. VAT-compliant handling of overseas seller stock, with full HMRC audit trail.

Food storage & distribution

Registered for the storage and distribution of ambient food products. Temperature monitoring, expiry tracking, allergen-aware pick workflows.

How it works

From your first SKU to same-day dispatch — three steps.

Step 01

Onboard your SKUs

Ship us your inventory. We inspect, count and rack every item against your ASN — usually within 48 hours of arrival.

Step 02

Sync your store

Direct API integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon and the major courier networks. Live orders flow to our WMS in real time.

Step 03

We pick, pack, ship

Same-day dispatch on orders placed before 3pm. Branded unboxing, tracking updates from your domain, 99.9% accuracy.
