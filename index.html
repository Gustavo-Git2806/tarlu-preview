How it works

From your first SKU to same-day dispatch — three steps.

Step 01

Onboard your SKUs

Ship us your inventory. We inspect, count and rack every item against your ASN — usually within 48 hours of arrival.

Step 02

Sync your store

Direct API integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon and the major courier networks. Live orders flow to our WMS in real time.

Step 03

We pick, pack, ship

Same-day dispatch on orders placed before 3pm. Branded unboxing, tracking updates from your domain, 99.9% accuracy.

Who we are

Fulfilment built around people.

Tarlu combines industry-leading logistics technology with a people-first approach. We help international brands, growing ecommerce businesses and local operators streamline their supply chain — so they can focus on what they do best.
